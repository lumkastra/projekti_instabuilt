Our story

From one family's workshop
to a two-continent builder.

InstaBuilt grew from a simple conviction: housing can be produced like a precision product — better quality, far less waste, and dramatically faster than conventional construction. Here is how that conviction became a company.

The founding

A family-built company

InstaBuilt was founded by Besnik Pllana and built under the Pllana Capital umbrella — a group that combines real estate, construction technology and global project management. The company remains family-led to this day.

The factory

140,000 m² of production

One production plot, one in-house factory and a 5,700 m² Insta Innovation Center — where our panelized and modular systems are engineered, tested and built under a single roof.

Germany

Aster Domo in Wertingen

Our Aster Domo 4-bedroom marvel in Wertingen and the multistory double house at Am Wildfrauhaus demonstrate what serial construction delivers on German soil — KfW40-grade, fast and affordable.

United States

Whisper Valley, Austin, Texas

InstaBuilt and AsterDomo arrived in the U.S. at Whisper Valley in Austin, Texas — bringing sustainable, millennial-focused living to the American market.

Communities

Cherry Communities

Our sustainable community brand — unveiled at the World of Modular fair and showcased at Blueprint booth 407 — turns single projects into connected, green neighborhoods.

On the road

From Davos to Las Vegas

UIA 2023, EXPO REAL 2023 & 2024, House of Kosova in Davos, Berlin Blockchain Week 2025, the Crypto Valley Conference in Zug, Blueprint Las Vegas 2024 and the Housing Innovation Summit in Denver — we exhibit where the industry decides the future of building.
